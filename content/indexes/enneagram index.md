Information about the Enneagram is scattered across a variety of sources, ranging from various thinkers and books to community-held discussions and threads. I originally started this page because my bookmarks were getting cluttered, and I hope to make it a little bit easier for people who want to learn more about the Enneagram to find useful resources.

Because there are so many sources of information, the best way to learn about the Enneagram is to consume from a variety of them and draw your own conclusions. The Enneagram is a theory that is forever developing as the people in the community bring their heads together.
