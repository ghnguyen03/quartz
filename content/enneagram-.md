The Enneagram is a personality theory structured around a diagram of nine types.

Each type, instead of being focused on lists of surface-level traits or characteristics, is defined instead by core misbeliefs, coping mechanisms, fears, and motivations. 

This theory is best used as a framework for cultivating **self-awareness** and **growth**. There are many behaviors and patterns of thought that we act out automatically, and many ways in which we limit ourselves without realizing. The Enneagram is a useful tool for understanding yourself and the reasons you do things.

On top of that, learning about the different types encourages you to think about why *other* people think or act in the ways that they do.
